# Historical results source

`results.csv` was downloaded from the public `martj42/international_results` repository:

```text
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/martj42/international_results/master/results.csv
```

At download time it contained 49,477 rows from 1872-11-30 through 2026-06-27. Future 2026 World Cup fixtures in this file use `NA` scores and are treated as unplayed.
