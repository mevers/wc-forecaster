# Historical World Cup squad sources

Historical squad CSVs are bootstrapped from Wikipedia squad pages:

```text
https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2018_FIFA_World_Cup_squads
https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_FIFA_World_Cup_squads
```

The `league` column uses the club's national association / league-system country from the squad table's club flag, not the exact domestic division.
